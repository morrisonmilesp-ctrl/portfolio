AgTech Invasive Species Removal

Senior Design · Team Lead · Lafayette College · Aug 2025 - May 2026

Overview

An AgTech platform that detects and removes invasive species contaminating vineyard harvests. I led an 11-member team from stakeholder outreach and fieldwork through alpha and beta prototypes to a final stakeholder demonstration.

My role

  • Organized team meetings, stakeholder outreach, biological research, and fieldwork.
  • Built tools to identify and address at-risk workstreams.
  • Took ownership of critical gaps: actuation, system power, and full-system integration.

Computer vision

  • Developed a YOLO 26 model to identify the pest.
  • Procured and curated 4k+ training images with 12k annotations.
  • Results: 90% mAP50, 91.2% precision, 83% recall.

Hardware and integration

  • Designed a custom 80/20 rack-and-pinion actuator that extends pneumatic coverage through a 12 in stroke under a 1 lb load, enabling positioned targeting of pests.
  • Specced and implemented remote power for the full platform.
  • Resolved all major full-system integration errors to deliver an operational demonstration to stakeholders.
